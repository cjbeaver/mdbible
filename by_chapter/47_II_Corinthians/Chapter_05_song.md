[Verse]
For in this tent we groan,
    longing to put on our heavenly dwelling,
if indeed by putting it on we may not be found naked,
    For while we are still in this tent,

[pre-chorus]
we persuade others,
    But what we are is known to God,
and I hope it is known also to your conscience,
    We are not commending ourselves to you again but giving you cause to boast about us,

[Chorus]

    be reconciled to God,
We implore you on behalf of Christ,
    For we know that if the tent that is our earthly home is destroyed, we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.
